# Commission the current house standard

These are Gomnee editorial defaults, not claims about search-engine ranking
requirements. Use the connected server's current guidance and findings for the
applicable standard and the exact accepted brief. Commission useful substance
that meets those commitments; do not quietly treat a shortfall as optional.

| Commitment                 | House default                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Answer first               | A useful answer in the opening approximately 100 words.                                                                    |
| Depth                      | A 1,200-word floor, the accepted target and retained top-five median when available; tutorials normally 1,800–3,000 words. |
| Contextual inventory links | 3–5 relevant existing pages, each placed in its reviewed section.                                                          |
| Inbound suggestions        | 2–3 existing source pages with complete anchor sentences, delivered as the owner's separate checklist.                     |
| Secondary terms            | 2–6 relevant terms from retained research, each in its commissioned H2 or body location.                                   |
| Primary phrase repetition  | Exact-match density at most 1.5%; the same primary phrase in at most one H2.                                               |
| Commissioned FAQ           | 4–6 genuinely sourced questions with self-contained answers of 40–80 words.                                                |
| Search title               | At most 60 Unicode characters and the reviewed primary phrase.                                                             |
| Search description         | 140–160 Unicode characters and the reviewed primary phrase.                                                                |
| Paragraphs                 | At most 4 sentences.                                                                                                       |
| Heading spacing            | Useful headings within approximately 300 words.                                                                            |

Write the reviewed primary phrase naturally in the title, slug, intended H1,
search description and opening. Plan H2 terms in the brief headings when useful;
a permitted relevant H2 in draft Markdown can also fulfil the commissioned
placement without changing accepted section headings. Follow current findings.
Match the tool's locale-aware counting;
the density limit is phrase occurrences divided by visible prose words, not a
target to increase repetition. Search title and description are explicit fields
and need not be identical to the Article title and summary.

Coverage and contribution require reasoning, not just counts. Retain the exact
query/market SERP and complete bodies for the top five organic results where
parity or relative novelty is judged. Relevant subtopics occurring in three of
five inform the coverage plan. Missing bodies stay unassessed; snippets cannot
prove parity. Fulfil the accepted questions and demonstrate the contribution
in the public Article.

Tutorials/explainers normally need a useful visual. Use real available media
with rights, alternative text and helpful context. Every major factual section
needs suitable primary-source support. Use real accountable authorship, a
profile and relevant credential; an organizational byline requires the human's
explicit attribution decision. Volatile tools and procedures need truthful
verification context and pinned covered versions.
Check tutorial verification commitments at brief preflight, before drafting.
First-hand support needs an actual run with date, environment, covered versions
and retained command/output or screenshot, plus a useful reviewed public
excerpt. A captured documentation page or private evidence alone is not a
publishable demonstration. If no suitable run exists, keep that limitation
explicit and follow the docs-only disclosure and exact authorized exception path described in [editorial-standard.md](editorial-standard.md).

## Exceptions are reviewed decisions

Reader task, format, locale, sparse inventory and genuine technical quotations
may justify an editorial exception. Record the concrete reason, affected
commitment and evidence gap in the proposed brief or revision, and follow the
server's human or explicitly policy-delegated review path described in
[editorial-standard.md](editorial-standard.md). A reason in an ordinary proposal
payload does not grant an exception. Preserve the exact standard, brief, revision and evidence basis;
`ready (override)` remains distinguishable from ordinary readiness.

Never fabricate links, questions, bylines, media or tests, lower an accepted
target silently, or pad the prose. Privacy, lineage, stale authority and
misleading public delivery cannot be overridden. An incomplete draft remains
saveable and reviewable, with named findings. Deterministic checks establish
decidable properties; independent editorial review and an authorized exact decision
establish useful coverage, supported claims, contribution and writing quality.
