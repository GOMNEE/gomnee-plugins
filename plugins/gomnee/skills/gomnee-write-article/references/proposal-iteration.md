# Repair saved Article work

Use the connected tool definitions and the [MCP workflow](../../../references/mcp-workflow.md).
Continue the person's authorized work from its saved state. A correction saves
a new proposal; the existing proposal and human decisions retain their history.

## Inspect the exact proposal

Read its durable decision and applicability first. A pending proposal can be
outdated; waiting will not repair its basis. Read fresh owning Article context
before preparing changed work.

When available, `gomnee_get_article_proposal_context` gives the bounded saved
proposal overview and its named parts. Select only the parts needed to assess
the requested change with `gomnee_read_article_proposal_part`. Copy the exact
proposal digest and advertised whole-part digest on the first read and every
continuation. Use returned section keys and offsets; do not guess selectors or
combine different proposal versions. Preserve truncation and omitted findings
as limitations. Read more when they can affect the decision.
The owning proposal's private-source permissions still apply to a small part
read. Follow the returned consent recovery rather than using a smaller read to
work around missing access.

For a cached connection without these tools, use `gomnee_read_article_proposal`
and its advertised read behavior. Do not ask the person to paste a large JSON
document into chat. If the host cannot expose necessary content, explain the
specific gap and return the exact saved review link.

## Amend within the returned form

Use `gomnee_amend_article_proposal` only when advertised and when its current
guidance permits the source proposal and requested change. Copy the exact
source proposal ID/digest, fresh owning context digest and supported amendment
form. Existing section and claim keys identify what changes; unsupported
additions, removals or reordering need the ordinary complete proposal form.
There is no arbitrary JSON patch operation.

Inspect the affected content before replacing it. Preserve untouched fields,
rich content, evidence and commitments. Include new retained receipts only
through the tool's advertised fields; attaching evidence does not establish
corroboration. Reuse a submission ID only for an identical retry. After an
ambiguous result, read durable status before submitting another mutation.

The result is a new ordinary proposal with its own exact review. The source
proposal stays unchanged. Summarize the correction and remaining findings,
show the returned review link or supported card, and continue after the usual
recorded decision. A new amendment proposal may apply under workspace settings
and separate application consent; read its actual receipt before continuing.
Amendment does not grant final Article approval or publication.

## Repair the named finding

Treat the report's exact path as a diagnostic location, not permission to write
that path. For a FAQ finding, read the affected question and answer, its accepted
brief plan and supporting evidence. Match the exact returned FAQ key or question;
fix that answer in the supported form instead of changing unrelated FAQs or
section prose. When the question or commission needs to change, propose the
brief correction first. Preserve the distinction between a missing commissioned
FAQ, a question not in the accepted plan, a short answer and unsupported content.
When the advertised form replaces the whole FAQ or another nested field,
read that complete field and preserve every unchanged item; an amendment does
not recursively merge an incomplete replacement.

For a tutorial, check whether an actual run and a suitable public excerpt exist.
Retained documentation cannot become first-hand output. Keep verification date,
environment, covered versions and evidence truthful. If no publishable run
exists, follow the returned docs-only disclosure and human exception guidance;
never claim an agent assessment is a completed human check.

## Retain a draft after an evidence-only brief repair

A conservative evidence-only correction can leave the accepted reader task,
structure and writing commitments intact. Propose that correction through the
normal brief review. Gomnee decides whether the exact change is eligible to
retain the existing draft; do not infer eligibility from your explanation.

After an eligible brief is applied by a human or under workspace settings, read fresh context. Confirm Gomnee
retained the complete draft as a new revision, including its rich content and
evidence, before continuing. That revision is unapproved and needs the ordinary
Article quality and a fresh final approval decision; earlier approval does not transfer. There is
no reason to reconstruct or resubmit an already retained complete draft.

Changes to the reader task, section plan, claim wording or other semantic commitments
need rework under the new brief. Follow the returned guidance and repair the
affected draft. Neither carrying content forward nor applying a brief establishes
that new evidence corroborates the Article's claims.
