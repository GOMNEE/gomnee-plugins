# Writing for the market

The accepted market in Gomnee's context decides the Article's language and
conventions. Write natively for that market; do not translate an English
article in your head.

## Localize, do not translate

- Use the phrases people in that market actually search for. They come from
  the Demand's keywords and saved research, not from translating an English
  keyword. Different languages phrase the same need differently and with
  different intent.
- Choose examples, references, laws, institutions, platforms and prices that
  exist in that market.
- Use local number, date, currency and unit formats — for Italy: `1.234,56 €`,
  `26 settembre 2026`, metric units.
- Keep product and brand names in their original form. Keep a technical term
  in English only when the local audience really uses it that way.
- Match the company's register. Many Italian brands address readers with
  "tu"; professional services often prefer an impersonal or "Lei" register.
  Follow the reviewed writing approach. If approved guidance is absent, propose
  a specific provisional approach in the brief rather than inventing a company
  voice or adding a confirmation step for every drafting request.
- Never mix languages on one page, including headings, image descriptions,
  the summary and the slug.

## Sources in another language

Sources may be in English while the Article is Italian. Cite them as they are;
paraphrase their content in the Article's language; never present a
translated quotation as the original words.

## Several languages

Gomnee publishes one Article per accepted Asset in one language. If the site
also serves other languages, the site handles language alternates. Do not
put hreflang-style alternates or second-language copies inside the Article.
