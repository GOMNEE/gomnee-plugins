# Research

Research in Gomnee is evidence attached to the Strategy. This skill covers the research that decides **what to write**:

| Part | Question it answers | Reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keywords | How do people phrase this Demand, and how many search for it? | [keywords.md](references/keywords.md) |
| Search results | What kind of page ranks, and what is missing from it? | [search-results.md](references/search-results.md) |

Gathering sources for one specific article belongs to the `article` skill.

Read [Working with Gomnee](../../references/working-with-gomnee.md) first, and [collecting.md](references/collecting.md) before any paid collection.

## Steps

1. **Read what is already saved.** `gomnee_list_saved_research` and `gomnee_list_research_evidence` show earlier research; read the relevant items before proposing anything new. Reading never costs money.
2. **Pick the part** from the person's question and load its reference. Keywords come first: the search-results check works from the keywords linked to each Demand.
3. **Agree the spend.** Quote every collection, tell the person the total and start only with their agreement.
4. **Collect, read and decide** as the reference describes.
5. **Record the outcome** where the next step can use it: keywords linked to Demands through a reviewed proposal, findings saved as an investigation.

## Rules that hold for all research

- Every unknown value is written "unknown", never 0 and never an estimate.
- Search Console impressions are not search volume.
- Search results show what ranks. They do not prove a factual claim.
- A keyword is linked to a Demand only if an article should rank for it. Volume does not change that.
- Tell the person plainly what the numbers mean for expectations before planning.

## What the person decides

- The spend, before any collection starts.
- Borderline keywords, and whether two Demands should share one article.
- The angle, when more than one gap is open.

## Hand-off

Give `plan`, per Demand: the primary keyword, the secondary keywords, the format that ranks, the angle, any Demand that becomes a section of another article, and the spend so far.
